# Portfolio Craft Kaizen Operation — 2026-09-04

## Scope and authority

This operation upgrades the eleven-engine portfolio so that its products are
made in deliberate, inspectable increments, with refinement and evidence at
each meaningful checkpoint. In scope: SRS, business plans, websites, social
media, Linux, proposals, general engineering, accounting doctrine, design,
digital research, and Windows administration.

The user's quality brief is the observation and product signal. It is not used
as evidence for current platform, market, legal, safety, or standards claims.
The Digital Research `source-evaluation` and `source-verification` skills were
read before standardisation. Currentness preflight: `NO_TIME_SENSITIVE_CLAIMS`;
the change admits a durable workflow contract only. Review date: 2026-10-04.

## Observe and baseline

The portfolio already had strong but uneven ingredients: anti-slop gates in
several engines, capped kaizen audits, source currentness rules, visual QA in
the website engine, and delivery evidence expectations in the engineering
engine. The recurring gap was operational: the principle of incremental craft
was not a single discoverable contract, and no cross-engine check proved that
every router carried it.

## Select

Root cause selected: the portfolio had quality principles distributed across
engines, but no shared, machine-checked contract forcing the same small-slice
craft loop and evidence vocabulary at every router.
